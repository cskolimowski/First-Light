# First Light — Canonical Art Direction

This document locks the visual language established by the approved character concept and gameplay mockup.

## Core identity

First Light is a newly born blue-white flame spirit. The world should feel ancient, quiet, slightly lonely, and beautiful rather than grim. The character is the brightest thing in the scene: a small source of warmth and hope moving through a cool nocturnal landscape.

## Character rules

- Silhouette: compact, round, instantly readable, topped by a tapered living flame.
- Body: white core with cyan/ice-blue glow and deeper blue at the flame tips.
- Face: very large glossy navy eyes, tiny mouth, soft friendly proportions.
- Scarf: deep cobalt/navy with subtle starlight specks and a small pale star clasp.
- Motion language: gentle float when idle, bouncy run, stretch upward on jump, slight widen on fall, soft squash on landing.
- The scarf should always reinforce motion by trailing behind the character.
- Small blue particles can shed from the flame to make First Light feel alive.

## World palette

Primary environment colors are midnight navy, cobalt, desaturated blue stone, cool moonlight, and muted teal foliage. Warm gold is reserved mainly for collectibles and key points of interest so it reads immediately against the cool world.

## Environment language

- Moonlit sky with stars and soft atmospheric haze.
- Strong parallax depth: mountains, ruins, forests, and mist at different speeds.
- Platforms read as old dark stone blocks with moss and occasional tiny luminous plants.
- Avoid bright daytime greens and orange-brown dirt as the dominant look.
- Foreground and background should stay low-contrast enough that First Light remains the focal point.

## Collectibles

Collectibles are warm golden stars/light fragments. They should glow softly and provide the main warm-color counterpoint to First Light's blue-white flame.

## Enemies

Enemies should feel native to the dark world rather than grotesque: compact shadow-creatures with indigo spines or fur and one or two luminous eyes. Their silhouettes should remain simple enough to read while moving.

## Goal object

The end-of-level goal is a blue luminous portal set inside an ancient stone arch. It should feel like a destination worth reaching, with enough bloom and contrast to remain visible from nearby gameplay space.

## Interface

UI follows the same midnight-blue / pale-blue / gold language. Panels are translucent and restrained. Serif display typography gives the game a storybook quality, while small system text stays clean and readable.

## Guiding phrase

> A new flicker. A new journey.

Every visual choice should support the idea that something tiny and hopeful has just come alive in a much older world.
