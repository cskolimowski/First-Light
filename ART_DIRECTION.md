# First Light — Visual Overhaul

This pass changes presentation only. Core movement, level geometry, collectibles, enemies, scoring, and win conditions remain intact.

## Art direction

A luminous storybook world at golden hour: layered atmospheric mountains, mist, soft clouds, distant forest silhouettes, textured earth, mossy grass, flowers, glowing collectibles, expressive creatures, a warm adventurer character, drifting motes, bloom-like glows, and a radiant end beacon.

## Rendering strategy

Everything remains dependency-free and generated with Canvas primitives, gradients, procedural texture marks, layered silhouettes, particles, and subtle animation. No external art assets are required.

## Goal

Make the exact same prototype feel dramatically more authored and alive without changing its game rules.
